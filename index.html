Startup · Agentic AI for Finance Research

Synapse Trading

Synapse Trading is the venture I’m building around agentic AI for quantitative finance research — turning planner/orchestrator agents, MCP-backed data layers, and code retrieval into a workbench that compresses the research loop from idea to evaluated strategy.

The bet: research teams should spend their time on hypotheses and execution, not on glue code, dataset wrangling, or rebuilding the same backtest scaffolding. Synapse focuses on the substrate that makes agentic research reliable enough to trust with real workflows.
